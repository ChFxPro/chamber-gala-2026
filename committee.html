Production Timeline

From Theme Approval To Event Night

Dates can tighten as the committee confirms the venue, sponsor deadlines, print vendor, and ticket launch.

  1. Theme and sponsor structure Confirm board direction, tier names, Local Legend treatment, and public launch language.
  2. Vendor commitments Approve catering, venue wording, entertainment/AV, printing, and decor scope.
  3. Sponsor sales window Launch sponsor page, send outreach copy, collect logos, and close recognition inventory.
  4. Ticket launch Publish final ticket pricing, venue, start time, purchase link, and guest-facing details.
  5. Print and production lock Finalize sponsor poster, Local Legend plates, program, signage, stage visuals, and run-of-show.
  6. Event week Confirm vendor arrivals, sponsor recognition, setup sequence, volunteer roles, and teardown plan.
