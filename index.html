Sponsor Visibility Principles

Creative Design Should Elevate The Sponsor

The theme gives the Chamber a strong visual system, but sponsor readability stays first.

Sponsor logos remain readable. Hierarchy is clear at a glance. Large empty white boxes are avoided when possible. Logos sit inside themed frames, panels, plates, badges, or display structures. Creative details support the sponsor, not overpower it. Local Legend posters make $250 sponsors feel special, not small.
